What the river is telling us this year.

Since 2024, Riverkeep Trust has restored riparian habitat, reduced flood risk, and created local jobs along four sites on the Nkosana River. This dashboard tracks what's actually changed — for residents, for the river, and for the funders making it possible.

Riverkeep Trust works with the City of Rivermead on nature-based interventions along the Nkosana River — restoring riparian buffers, removing debris and invasive species, and rebuilding the wetland's capacity to absorb floodwater before it reaches homes downstream. The figures below come from our own field monitoring across four active sites and are updated quarterly.
